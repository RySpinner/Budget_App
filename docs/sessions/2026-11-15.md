## Fix: manual income entries stored with the wrong sign
- Owner report: interest entered by hand in a savings account with no CSV "wasn't counted" on the Accounts page.
- Cause: the manual-entry form stored the typed amount as-is. In the database's sign convention a positive amount is money out, so "5.00" of interest *lowered* the balance by $5 (reconciliation off by $10, Dashboard income −$5). Transfer legs created by the app were fine.
- Fix: `transactions.manual_amount_cents` flips the sign for income categories, so amounts are typed the way you'd say them; the form's help text says so. Regression tests cover the helper and the form, entering interest into a savings account with no CSV.
