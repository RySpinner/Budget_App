## Manual interest entry "not counted" (investigation)
- Owner report: interest entered by hand in a savings account with no CSV didn't seem to count on the Accounts page.
- First guess (wrong): income entered as a positive number is stored as money out. The form was changed to flip the sign for income categories. The owner had in fact entered interest as a negative number (correct), so that change was reverted the same day. The form's help text now states the convention: money in is negative, as in the table.
- Other likely causes, which the data has to confirm:
  1. the entry is dated on or before the account's opening balance date, so it's treated as already included in the opening balance;
  2. the entry went into Cash, the form's default account.
- To make cause 1 visible, the Accounts page now notes how many transactions fall on or before the opening balance date (`balances.rows_before_opening`).
- DECISIONS.md #138–139. 379 tests passing.
- Outcome: the owner had entered the interest in the wrong account.
